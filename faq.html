Travel FAQ

Answers to common questions about European travel and our site.

Do I need a Visa for Europe?

It depends on your citizenship. The Schengen Area covers most countries, allowing visa-free travel for many tourists for up to 90 days.

What is the best time to visit?

Spring (April-June) and Fall (September-October) offer the best weather and fewer crowds compared to the peak summer season.

How does the Euro Dash game work?

It's a fun mini-game to play while waiting for trains! Your high scores are saved locally in your browser.

Is the site free?

Yes, all our travel guides and the Euro Dash game are completely free to use.

Can I contribute a guide?

We love community stories! Contact us via the blog section to submit your travel experience.
